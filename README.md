# Apparel & Textiles Strategic Business Analysis

## 📌 Overview

An end-to-end **Apparel & Textiles Business Analytics** project that transforms sales data into meaningful business insights using Python.

## 🎯 Objectives

* Analyze sales and revenue trends
* Identify top-performing product categories
* Compare regional performance
* Analyze discount impact
* Calculate key business KPIs
* Forecast future sales
* Generate strategic business recommendations

## 🔄 Methodology

```text
Data Collection
      ↓
Data Cleaning
      ↓
EDA
      ↓
Visualization
      ↓
KPI Analysis
      ↓
Forecasting
      ↓
Business Insights
      ↓
Strategic Recommendations
```

## 🛠️ Technologies

* Python

## 📊 Key Analysis

* Monthly Revenue Trends

## 💡 Key Insights

The analysis helps identify high-performing products and regions, seasonal demand patterns, discount effects, and future sales trends. These insights support better production, inventory, marketing and pricing decisions.

## 🚀 Recommendations

* Adopt demand-based production planning
* Optimize inventory according to demand
* Use targeted discounts
* Localize regional product strategies
* Monitor KPIs through analytics dashboards
* Use forecasting for future planning
